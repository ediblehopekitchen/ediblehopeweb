Our story

Every weekday morning, volunteers prepare meals, pour coffee, and build community with our unhoused and low-income neighbors in Ballard. We're open Monday to Friday, 8–10 AM, all year long.

For 38 years, Edible Hope served breakfast from the basement of St. Luke's Episcopal Church. Today the kitchen operates from the lower level of St. Paul's UCC at 6512 12th Ave NW, with the same promise: daily, no-barrier hot meals that are open to all.

Edible Hope Kitchen is a ministry of St. Luke's Episcopal Church, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We serve more than 40,000 meals a year with help from nearly 200 volunteers.

Our team

Nick Gardner

Program Director

Nick believes we share responsibility for meeting our neighbors' needs, and he especially loves doing it through food. He previously ran operations for a sustainable agriculture company and spent nearly two years mediating and teaching in middle schools. Edible Hope lets him combine service, hands-on community work, and a better food system.

nick@stlukesseattle.org

Andy Basinger

Community Manager

Andy looks after the safety and well-being of our guests, volunteers, neighbors, and staff. He holds a Social Science degree and a mentorship certificate, has de-escalation training from the Seattle Police Department, and brings experience in security, nonprofits, and foster homes.

andy@stlukesseattle.org

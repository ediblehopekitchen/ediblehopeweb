Ways to give

Gifts of every size help. Choose whatever works best for you.

Double your gift

Many employers match donations through Benevity or similar programs. Search for St. Luke's Episcopal Church, Seattle, and choose Edible Hope Kitchen.

Give in someone's honor

To make a gift in honor of someone, email their name and contact information to meals@stlukesseattle.org and we'll let them know.

Donate food

We accept food donations case by case, so please email us before dropping anything off. Money goes furthest, since we buy exactly what the menu needs.

Edible Hope Kitchen is a ministry of St. Luke's Episcopal Church, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

The Fall Fundraiser

Every October, our community gathers for an evening that funds a year of breakfasts. Our most recent fundraiser, at Old Stove Brewery in Ballard, brought together more than 150 guests and raised over $150,000.

The next fundraiser is planned for October 2027. Newsletter subscribers get the first invitation.

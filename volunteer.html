Spend a morning in our kitchen.

We need help Monday to Friday, 7–11 AM, cooking, prepping, serving, and cleaning. No experience needed.

Sign up for a shift Bring a group

How to volunteer

  1. Pick a shift

    Choose a weekday morning on our SignUpGenius page, or email Nick at meals@stlukesseattle.org.

  2. Arrive by 7 AM

    Come to the lower level of St. Paul's UCC, 6512 12th Ave NW, Seattle, WA 98117.

  3. Cook, serve, and connect

    Staff will set you up with a task, whether that's prepping food, cooking, serving guests, or cleaning up. Shifts wrap up by 11 AM.

Volunteer roles

  • Cooking
  • Food prep
  • Serving meals and coffee
  • Cleaning
Sign up for a shift

Volunteer as a group

Teams, congregations, schools, and friends are welcome. We host groups of 2–20 people on weekday mornings, 7–11 AM.

First-time groups should arrive before 7:30 AM for a short orientation before we open to the public.

Email Nick to book a date

Questions

Do I need kitchen experience?

No. Staff will match you with a task that fits your skills and comfort level.

Where do I go when I arrive?

Head to the lower level of St. Paul's UCC at 6512 12th Ave NW, Seattle, WA 98117. If you get lost, call 206-508-5497.

Can I just stop by?

You're always welcome to stop by for coffee. To volunteer, please sign up first so we can plan the morning.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Email Nick Gardner, our Program Director, at meals@stlukesseattle.org.

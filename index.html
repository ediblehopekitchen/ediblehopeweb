A hot breakfast and a warm welcome, every weekday.

Edible Hope Kitchen serves free, chef-made meals to anyone who walks through our door in Ballard. We feed people in mind, body, and spirit.

Open weekdays 8–10 AM Free hot breakfast. Everyone is welcome.

What's for breakfast

Each week we recover food from local grocery stores and plan a menu around it, with dietary accommodations every day.

  • Monday to ThursdayRotating chef-made mains like teriyaki chicken, beef curry, meatloaf, and lemon-butter salmon
  • FridayOur famous pancakes and eggs

We build community through a shared meal, love, and service.

For 38 years, breakfast was served in the basement of St. Luke's. Today we cook at St. Paul's UCC, just a few blocks away, with the same open door: no barriers, no paperwork, and help for physical, mental health, dietary, and other needs.
